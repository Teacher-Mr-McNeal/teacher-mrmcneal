Academy Overview

The Galena Outdoor Leadership & Community Stewardship Academy is a year-long experiential learning program designed specifically for Interior Alaska.

Students develop leadership, subsistence skills, outdoor competence, risk management abilities, community service habits, and workforce readiness.
