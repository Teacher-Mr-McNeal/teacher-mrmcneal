Academy Overview

The Galena Outdoor Leadership & Community Stewardship Academy is a year-long experiential learning program designed specifically for Interior Alaska. The academy combines outdoor leadership, service learning, subsistence skills, winter survival, technical skills, project management, and career readiness through field-based experiences and community projects.
