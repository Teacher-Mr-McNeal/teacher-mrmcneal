About the Author

Ryan McNeal is an educator and experiential learning specialist whose work focuses on outdoor education, place-based learning, expedition design, curriculum innovation, and student-centered approaches to teaching and learning. His projects span both Alaska and international educational contexts, supporting schools in creating meaningful opportunities for students to learn through exploration, service, reflection, and authentic engagement with communities and the natural world.
