Wilderness First Responder Curriculum & Patient Scenario Card System

Galena City School District - IDEA • BASE Medical (Denver, Colorado) • Wilderness Medicine Education • 2027

Developed and co-taught a comprehensive Wilderness First Responder (WFR) intensive for eighteen Alaska students ages 14-17 through a collaboration between Galena City School District, the Interior Distance Education for Alaska (IDEA), BASE Medical, and myself. As part of the project, a large curriculum dataset of progressive patient scenario cards was designed to support skill development across every stage of the WFR program. Scenarios were intentionally aligned to course sequencing, allowing students to practice patient assessments, trauma management, environmental emergencies, wilderness medicine decision-making, and leadership skills through increasingly complex simulations.

The patient simulation library was created as an instructional resource to support experiential learning and scenario-based wilderness medical training for secondary students pursuing advanced outdoor education and emergency response skills.
